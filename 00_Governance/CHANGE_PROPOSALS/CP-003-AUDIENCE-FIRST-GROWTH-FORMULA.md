# CP-003 — Audience-First Growth Formula

Status: `APPROVED / IMPLEMENTED`

Effective: 2026-09-01

## Problem

The project had accumulated historical distribution-first and premature money-first decisions that could conflict with the latest audience-growth strategy.

## Decision

1. relevant follower growth is the current POC North Star;
2. monetization follows audience leverage;
3. Stage A = reply-led cold start, Stage B = reply/original dual engine, Stage C = original-content-led owned distribution;
4. Day 3/7/10/15/30 follower KPI in `OPERATING_KPI.md` v3.0 are active;
5. repeatable growth-formula discovery is a first-class objective;
6. personal Feishu is the only authorized production signal channel;
7. `PROJECT_SPEC.md` v2.0 is the top-level single source of truth.

## Test

Run the 30-day audience-first experiment from the human-verified personal-Feishu start, collect action/outcome variables, evaluate KPI, and promote a formula only after repeated evidence.

## Supersedes

- old Day-30 ~20 follower direction signal;
- money-first early cash gates;
- group Feishu alerting;
- generic seven-day distribution proof as the final POC objective.
