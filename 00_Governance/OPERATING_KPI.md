# China Tech X Operating KPI — Follow-Reason v4.0

## 1. North Star

**Relevant follower growth created by recognizable value and point of view.**

Posting volume is not a KPI.

## 2. Primary Funnel

```text
STOP -> ENGAGE -> PROFILE INTEREST -> FOLLOW REASON -> RELEVANT FOLLOW
```

Track when available:

- impressions/views — distribution only;
- likes/replies/reposts/bookmarks — engagement;
- profile visits — identity curiosity;
- follower delta — conversion;
- qualitative evidence that users understand the account promise;
- topic/thesis patterns associated with relevant followers.

## 3. System-Health Metrics

Still track:

- runtime success rate;
- alert latency;
- review-worth precision;
- operator time;
- model-call/token budget.

These measure system quality, not account value.

## 4. Removed KPIs

No milestone requires:

- minimum reply count;
- minimum original-post count;
- minimum Article count;
- minimum daily posting cadence;
- minimum hotspot count.

## 5. Milestone Reviews

Follower milestones remain directional checkpoints, not output-quota gates:

- Day 3: 8 relevant followers target;
- Day 7: 15;
- Day 10: 25;
- Day 15: 40;
- Day 30: 100 target, 200 stretch.

If missed, diagnose in this order:

1. audience fit;
2. viewpoint distinctiveness;
3. stop/hook quality;
4. engagement/value;
5. profile/follow promise;
6. distribution/target selection;
7. discovery coverage;
8. runtime/tooling.

Never respond to weak follower growth by increasing content volume automatically.

## 6. Reply Interpretation

Reply impressions prove borrowed distribution only.

A reply becomes strategically valuable when it also creates one or more of:

- meaningful engagement;
- profile visits;
- relevant follows;
- a thesis worth turning into owned content;
- a relationship with a relevant creator/community.

## 7. Content Quality Review

For each original, record qualitatively:

- content bucket;
- clear thesis yes/no;
- evidence/practice yes/no;
- audience consequence;
- follow-reason strength;
- whether wording feels uniquely attributable to Kenny;
- whether an AI-template pattern was used.

## 8. Decision Rule

One strong, memorable post may be better than several weak posts.
No activity metric can override that rule.
